## Decision options

### GO

All of the following must be true:

- [ ] No unresolved P0 finding.
- [ ] No unresolved P1 finding.
- [ ] All critical safety-comprehension items ([safety-questionnaire.md](safety-questionnaire.md)) at 100% across Round 2 participants.
- [ ] All [success-criteria.md](success-criteria.md) thresholds met in Round 2, or documented exceptions accepted per the exceptions rule below.
- [ ] Pilot materials (participant-facing documents, facilitator guide) are complete and reflect any Round 1/Round 2 corrections.
- [ ] Evaluation data-governance approach ([data-governance.md](data-governance.md)) was followed without incident.
- [ ] Stage 12 packaging is justified by the evidence gathered — not merely assumed.

### CONDITIONAL GO

Applies when there is no unresolved safety blocker (all GO safety conditions above are met), but:

- [ ] One or more P2 findings remain, each with an explicit, time-bounded remediation plan recorded here.
- [ ] Or: one or more thresholds in [success-criteria.md](success-criteria.md) narrowly missed, with a documented, testable condition for re-evaluation before Stage 12 spend proceeds.

Every condition attached to a CONDITIONAL GO must state: what must change, by when, and how it will be re-verified. An open-ended "will improve later" condition is not acceptable.

### NO-GO

Triggered by any of:

- [ ] An unresolved safety misunderstanding (any critical safety-questionnaire item below 100%, unresolved).
- [ ] Core-task completion below the threshold in [success-criteria.md](success-criteria.md), unresolved.
- [ ] Low perceived relevance or usefulness (below threshold), unresolved.
- [ ] Inability to recruit participants matching the target-user profile in sufficient numbers.
- [ ] Inadequate privacy or consent arrangements identified during the evaluation.
- [ ] Insufficient evidence overall to justify Stage 12 packaging/deployment expenditure.

A NO-GO is a valid, complete Stage 11 outcome. It must be recorded as such — not reframed as a partial success or a disguised CONDITIONAL GO.
