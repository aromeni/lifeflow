# Stage 11 — Participant Screener (template)

**Status:** Draft template — not for distribution until [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md) authorises recruitment · **Date:** 2026-07-30

Companion: [stage-11-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11-plan.md) §2 · [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md)

**This screener must not be published or sent to any prospective participant during this task, or before recruitment is authorised.** It exists here so its content can be reviewed in advance.

## Screening questions

1. Are you 18 years of age or older? *(exclude if no)*
2. Does your work involve regularly coordinating tasks, requests, or deadlines through email and a calendar? *(exclude if no)*
3. Do you personally use Gmail (or a comparable email service you manage yourself, not through a dedicated assistant)? *(exclude if no)*
4. Do you personally use Google Calendar (or a comparable calendar you manage yourself)? *(exclude if no)*
5. Are you personally responsible for tracking your own follow-ups and commitments, rather than having someone else do this for you? *(exclude if no)*
6. How do you currently keep track of what needs your attention? *(open text — not an exclusion, used to confirm target-user fit)*
7. Are you available for a single session of approximately 45–60 minutes in the proposed evaluation window? *(exclude if no)*
8. Do you have any existing personal, commercial, or employment relationship with the person running this evaluation that might make it hard for you to give candid feedback? *(flag for review, not an automatic exclusion, but must be checked against [evaluation-context-decision.md](evaluation-context-decision.md) question 9)*
9. Would participating in this session require you to expose real content from your own Gmail or Calendar account? *(exclude if yes — the session uses only fictional demo data; this question exists to catch a participant's misunderstanding of that before the session, not to solicit their real content)*
10. Do you have any accessibility needs we should accommodate for this session (e.g., screen-reader compatibility, additional time, a preferred communication format)? *(optional, voluntary — not required to answer)*

## What this screener does not ask

No health information, ethnicity, religion, political views, biometric data, or other special-category data is requested at any point. If a prospective participant volunteers such information unprompted, the facilitator does not record it as part of the screening result.

Question 6 is open text and may surface an employer name or other identifying detail; the same anonymisation rule as session quotes applies (see [data-governance.md](data-governance.md)'s "Quote anonymisation" section) — added during the Round 1 desk rehearsal after noting the original screener draft did not say this explicitly.

## Distribution rule

This screener is not distributed to anyone until [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md) shows every applicable item satisfied.
