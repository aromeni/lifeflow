## What is this about?

You're invited to take part in a short evaluation session for LifeFlow, a tool that helps people keep track of things in their email and calendar. We want to find out whether the product is easy to understand and genuinely useful — there are no right or wrong answers, and we are testing the product, not you.

## What will I be asked to do?

You'll use a demo version of LifeFlow that only contains made-up, fictional data — no real email account or calendar of yours will be connected. A facilitator will ask you to complete a series of short tasks (such as finding out what LifeFlow thinks needs your attention, and reviewing a suggested action) while thinking aloud about what you see and expect. The session takes approximately [45–60] minutes.

## What will happen to what I say and do?

By default, sessions are **not recorded**. A facilitator takes written notes during the session. We collect: your answers and comments, timing of tasks, and brief demographic details relevant to whether you match the intended user group (see [data-governance.md](data-governance.md) for the full list). We do not collect your real email address, real Gmail or Calendar content, or any special-category personal data.

## Is my participation voluntary?

Yes. You can stop at any point without giving a reason, and you can ask for your session notes to be withdrawn and deleted up to [withdrawal deadline — set in data-governance.md] after your session, with no consequence to you.

## Will my data be identifiable?

No. Your notes are recorded against a participant ID (for example, `P-R1-03`), not your name. See [data-governance.md](data-governance.md) for exactly how your ID is generated, stored, and eventually deleted.

## What happens to the findings?

Findings are summarised in aggregate (e.g., "4 of 5 participants understood X") in an internal findings report ([findings-template.md](findings-template.md)) used to decide whether LifeFlow is ready for the next stage of development.
