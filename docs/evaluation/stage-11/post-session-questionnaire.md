# Stage 11 — Post-Session Questionnaire (template)

**Status:** Planning document · **Date:** 2026-07-30

Companion: [success-criteria.md](success-criteria.md) · [findings-template.md](findings-template.md)

Administered immediately after task completion, before the safety questionnaire or after it (facilitator's choice, kept consistent within a round).

## Rating items (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree)

1. I understood what the Today brief was telling me. *(brief comprehension)*
2. The item ranked highest was genuinely the one I'd deal with first. *(priority relevance)*
3. The reasons given for why something mattered were useful. *(explanation usefulness)*
4. The proposed actions I saw would save me effort in real life. *(proposed-action usefulness)*
5. I felt in control when reviewing what would happen before approving something. *(perceived control)*
6. I trust this product to do what it says it does. *(perceived trust)*
7. This product would be useful to me in my day-to-day work. *(perceived usefulness)*

## Usability measure

Administer a standard 10-item System Usability Scale (SUS), using the product's official wording, scored 0–100 per the standard SUS formula. (Reproduced by reference here rather than copied verbatim to avoid unreviewed relicensing of third-party questionnaire text — the facilitator uses the canonical published SUS item set.)

## Direct questions

8. Would you use LifeFlow again? (Yes / Probably / Probably not / No)
9. What's the one thing you'd change first?
10. Was there anything that confused or worried you?
11. Is there anything you expected LifeFlow to do that it didn't?
