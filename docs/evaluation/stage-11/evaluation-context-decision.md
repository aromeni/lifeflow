This document distinguishes two separate things that must not be conflated: **the current, active operational mode** (owner-only, no participants, decided below) and **the classification that will apply if and when external participants are later involved** (also decided below, but not yet activated — recruitment remains blocked regardless of this decision; see [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md)).

## Current operational mode

**OWNER-ONLY INTERNAL VALIDATION.**

This means, as of 2026-07-30:

- the project owner may test and use the system personally;
- synthetic data remains the default environment;
- automated, manual, resilience, and security testing may continue;
- owner observations may be recorded as engineering evidence (see [owner-observation-template.md](owner-observation-template.md));
- no external participant is involved in this mode;
- no participant-research evidence is being produced in this mode.

The full programme this mode covers is defined in [stage-11a-owner-validation-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11a-owner-validation-plan.md).

## Future participant-evaluation classification

**INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION — PUBLICATION-INTENDED**, recorded now as the anticipated route for if and when external participants are later involved — this classification is decided, but not activated: it does not authorise recruitment, contact, or data collection, all of which remain blocked by [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md).

Recorded facts underlying this classification:

- the project owner has completed their MSc;
- LifeFlow is not currently part of a university programme, assessment, dissertation, or supervisor-directed project;
- a future intention to pursue journal publication of participant findings does not, by itself, make the *current* owner-only work university-linked — publication intent and institutional affiliation are separate questions;
- before any participant is involved under this route, independent ethics considerations, a privacy/lawful-basis assessment (see [product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md](product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md)), and any target-journal's own requirements (e.g., its research-ethics or human-subjects disclosure standards) must be separately resolved — none of that resolution has happened yet;
- if a future institutional affiliation, formal collaboration, funding arrangement, or university research route becomes applicable, the UNIVERSITY-LINKED classification below would apply instead, and this record would need to be revisited at that time, not assumed to still hold.

## Public-communication constraint

Content documenting this work (for example, LinkedIn articles about the engineering journey, synthetic demonstrations, or the owner's own testing) may describe owner testing, synthetic scenarios, and engineering progress, but **must not describe owner-only internal validation as independent participant research, and must not claim participant findings, sample sizes, or research conclusions that do not exist.** This constraint applies regardless of which future participant-evaluation classification is eventually activated.

## Screening questions (for the future participant-evaluation classification)

The questions below informed the future-route classification above and remain the reference point if that classification needs revisiting (e.g., if an institutional affiliation later arises). They are not being re-answered from scratch for the current owner-only mode, which involves no participants and therefore does not implicate them.

## Screening questions

The project owner should answer every question below. An honest "not sure" is a valid answer and should be resolved before proceeding, not guessed at.

1. Will findings contribute to an MSc assignment, dissertation, or other academic assessment?
2. Will findings contribute to a PhD study or other university research?
3. Is a university supervisor directing or reviewing the evaluation?
4. Will University of Kent branding, facilities, systems, or participant pools be used?
5. Is publication, conference presentation, or an academic article contemplated?
6. Will findings be presented as research evidence (to a supervisor, examiner, journal, or conference)?
7. Is the work solely private product-development usability testing, with no academic or research framing?
8. Is there a commercial or employment relationship with participants?
9. Are participants colleagues, students, employees, clients, or people over whom the evaluator has influence?
10. Will identifiable personal data be collected (beyond a pseudonymous participant ID)?
11. Will audio, video, screenshots, or transcripts be recorded?
12. Will incentives be offered?
13. Will any participant be under 18 or potentially vulnerable?
14. Will any special-category data be requested, or is it likely to arise incidentally (health, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, biometric data, etc.)?
15. Will real Gmail or Calendar information be used at any point?

## Decision categories (for the future participant-evaluation classification)

### UNIVERSITY-LINKED

Applies if the answer to any of questions 1–6 becomes yes in future (it is currently no — the owner has completed their MSc and the project is not currently university-linked). Would require: confirmation of the applicable university ethics review route, supervisor sign-off, and any required institutional approval **before** recruitment or data collection begins. See [ethics-enquiry-summary.md](ethics-enquiry-summary.md) — the pack to send to the relevant ethics contact or supervisor, if this route later becomes applicable.

### INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION — selected as the future route

Questions 1–6 are currently all no, and the evaluation, if and when it involves participants, is intended as a deliberate, systematic usability study (the design already committed to in [stage-11-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11-plan.md): two rounds, fixed thresholds, structured tasks), potentially supporting a future journal publication. Requires, **before any recruitment**: a documented controller identity, privacy information, a reasoned lawful-basis decision, a retention policy, a participant process, and resolution of any target journal's own research/ethics disclosure requirements. See [product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md](product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md) — none of its open decisions are resolved by this selection; selecting the category starts that work, it does not finish it.

### INFORMAL FEEDBACK

Not selected. Not a substitute for INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION merely to avoid its requirements — **labelling systematic usability research "informal feedback" does not remove data-protection or ethical responsibilities.** If the two-round, 20-task, threshold-driven design in [stage-11-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11-plan.md) is what's actually run with participants, it is not informal feedback, regardless of what it is called.

### UNDECIDED

No longer the current state for the future-route classification (see Decision record below). Still describes any as-yet-unresolved future scenario not covered by the above (e.g., if an institutional affiliation later arises, this record must be revisited, not assumed to still say INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION).
