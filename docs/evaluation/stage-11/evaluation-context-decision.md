**This document does not select a category on the project owner's behalf.** It exists so the project owner can make an informed, explicit, recorded choice before any recruitment or data collection. Until a category is selected and recorded below, the evaluation context is **UNDECIDED** and recruitment remains blocked (see [recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md](recruitment-authorisation-checklist.md)).

## Screening questions

The project owner should answer every question below. An honest "not sure" is a valid answer and should be resolved before proceeding, not guessed at.

1. Will findings contribute to an MSc assignment, dissertation, or other academic assessment?
2. Will findings contribute to a PhD study or other university research?
3. Is a university supervisor directing or reviewing the evaluation?
4. Will University of Kent branding, facilities, systems, or participant pools be used?
5. Is publication, conference presentation, or an academic article contemplated?
6. Will findings be presented as research evidence (to a supervisor, examiner, journal, or conference)?
7. Is the work solely private product-development usability testing, with no academic or research framing?
8. Is there a commercial or employment relationship with participants?
9. Are participants colleagues, students, employees, clients, or people over whom the evaluator has influence?
10. Will identifiable personal data be collected (beyond a pseudonymous participant ID)?
11. Will audio, video, screenshots, or transcripts be recorded?
12. Will incentives be offered?
13. Will any participant be under 18 or potentially vulnerable?
14. Will any special-category data be requested, or is it likely to arise incidentally (health, ethnicity, religion, political opinion, biometric data, etc.)?
15. Will real Gmail or Calendar information be used at any point?

## Decision categories

### UNIVERSITY-LINKED

Selected when the answer to any of questions 1–6 is yes. Requires: confirmation of the applicable University of Kent ethics review route, supervisor sign-off, and any required institutional approval **before** recruitment or data collection begins. See [ethics-enquiry-summary.md](ethics-enquiry-summary.md) — the pack to send to the relevant ethics contact or supervisor.

### INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION

Selected when questions 1–6 are all no, but the evaluation is a deliberate, systematic usability study (the design already committed to in [stage-11-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11-plan.md): two rounds, fixed thresholds, structured tasks). Requires: a documented controller identity, privacy information, a reasoned lawful-basis decision, a retention policy, and a participant process — all before recruitment. See [product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md](product-evaluation-privacy-assessment.md).

### INFORMAL FEEDBACK

Selected only for genuinely minimal, non-recorded, non-systematic feedback — not a substitute for choosing INDEPENDENT PRODUCT EVALUATION merely to avoid its requirements. **Labelling systematic usability research "informal feedback" does not remove data-protection or ethical responsibilities.** If the two-round, 20-task, threshold-driven design in [stage-11-plan.md](../../delivery/stage-11-plan.md) is what's actually being run, it is not informal feedback, regardless of what it is called.

### UNDECIDED

The default and current state. No recruitment, participant contact, or data collection is authorised.
